# RTS Gangster 1 — Beta 0.3.0-beta.9

This beta builds on the **Hoods command bar**, building dispatch, and Illustrated 1930s vehicle artwork shipped in beta.8. The focus is a more consequential police, court and prison simulation.

## Gunfights and investigations

- Related shots from the same shooter within the active time and distance window are consolidated into one police file rather than opening a duplicate for every shot.
- Individual killers and additional victims still get their own homicide records.
- A wounding shot and later shots are tracked in the investigation, even when the firefight moves.
- Shootout fatalities can be charged as **murder during a shootout**.

## Court cases and the Case Desk

- Click a police file to open its **Case Desk**: case stage, charging decision, warrant, hearing, officer, prosecutor, judge, witnesses and jurors, subject to what your lawyer has learned.
- A lawyer can obtain investigation details and witness names. Undisclosed case notes remain private until the file is opened.
- Companion homicide files in a joined prosecution now link to the **same court case** in the Case Desk.
- Prosecutors may refuse or delay charges when evidence is insufficient; a bought prosecutor may suppress charges.
- A juror's influence is weighed against the strength of the evidence; hearings can be delayed while defence counsel is busy.

## Prison sentences and operations

- Convictions carry crime-dependent prison sentences: murder and homicide generally mean years, not a few weeks.
- Prison contacts and protection matter for jailed gang members. Protection can stop a prison attack.
- A fatal prison attack generates a criminal investigation and witness evidence rather than being an unreported game event.

## Release quality

- Rebuilt and checked the generated world-save schema for new case fields. Existing saves keep the established game identity and storage location.
- Added dedicated regression tests for separate gunfight files, combined homicide charges, bought prosecutors, and prison killings to the **Windows release test pipeline**. Existing prison, police, court and save-compatibility regressions also run.
- The release pipeline must pass its checks and produce **both Windows packages** before publication.

- `RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.9.exe` — run without installation.
