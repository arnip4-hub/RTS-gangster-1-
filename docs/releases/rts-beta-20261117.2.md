# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.2

This beta builds on the live police and court systems. The agreed 24-real-minute
game day and seven-day city calendar are unchanged.

## Police investigations and employment

- A lawyer's request to investigate a reported crime is now long-running file
  work. Progress accumulates gradually over roughly six in-game weeks and is
  saved with the campaign.
- The investigation continues through week boundaries and is not discarded by
  ordinary specialist-job cleanup. Its progress remains visible while it is
  underway.
- An active file investigation reserves the lawyer, preventing overlapping
  investigation or bribe jobs from using the same lawyer.
- Police officers hired by the gang remain employed and on weekly retainer
  until dismissed. Area recruitment capacity is counted per lawyer tour rather
  than as a lifetime cap.

## Business and property

- A damaged but standing commercial, industrial or tenement property can be
  purchased for 60% of its normal asking price (a 40% discount).
- The business remains closed after purchase. The new owner must still pay its
  listed repair cost before it can trade again.
- Destroyed properties remain unavailable for purchase.
- The property panel and order card show the discounted price and explain the
  outstanding repair cost.

## Windows files

- **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup-0.3.0-beta.2.exe** — Windows installer.
- **RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.2.exe** — portable Windows build.

Existing saves remain compatible. This is an experimental fan project based
on the separate remake source; it does not include assets from the 1998 GOG
game.
