# RTS gangster beta 0.2.0-beta.3 — Targeting, skills and map fixes

## Windows installation

Download **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup.exe** and close the game before running it.
**RTS-gangster-beta-Portable.exe** runs without installation. The app continues
to use the existing save folder. The source-code archives are for development.

## Fixes in this patch

- Clicking a purchased business no longer picks its former seller as the
  target of a Kill order. Civilian and family targeting remains available.
- Job cards reveal independent owners only after a visit or scouting, and
  show the current manager of an owned business. A refused extortion or
  intimidation visit now supplies legitimate owner/protection information.
- Snitch fees count at most six names. Quote and purchase use the same cap.
- Accountant and lawyer recruitment shows the same wage that hiring applies.
  Intelligence determines specialist quotes. Free specialists in older saves
  receive the corrected quote; existing employees keep their contracted pay.
- Order cards, staffing, work-time estimates and execution share the task-skill
  model: Raid uses Fists or Firearms, Kill uses Knives or Firearms, Smash Up and
  Assault use Fists, Torch uses Arson, Bomb uses Explosives, Intimidate uses
  Intimidation plus Fists, and Kidnap uses Intimidation plus Fists or Firearms.
  A firearm alternative requires that person to have an allocated gun. Busy
  crew members retain their weapon reservations.
- The larger surname pool and deterministic name allocation give newly
  generated people distinct full names, including family members. Existing
  saved names are retained; later recruits avoid names already in that save.
- Find excludes dead people. Choosing a known person or building centres the
  map; unlocated relatives remain hidden until discovered.
- City Plan preserves an existing zoom above the fitted-city view when
  centring on HQ or report links. Reviewing an order marks its actual sites.
- HQ terraces resolve to their street front, and 3D apartment outlines include
  the whole site. Map chooser tabs and selected rows are visibly marked.

## RTS and artwork

The 24-hour, seven-day calendar, continuous weekly settlement, civilian and
family systems, illustrated 3D buildings, character wardrobe and painted cars
continue in this build. The upstream 8 am–8 pm clock and week-based backup-crew
rules have not been adopted. Save import/export from upstream 1.0.7 is a
separate future addition.

## Verification

Targeted regression checks cover targeting and privacy, capped fees and wages,
skill resolution and finite equipment, names and save/restore, and site grouping.
The original-size RTS city check generates over 8,000 uniquely named people.
The Windows build workflow runs the focused regressions before packaging.
