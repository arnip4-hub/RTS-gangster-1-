# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.4

This update replaces the remake's score in the RTS beta with the music supplied
for this project by solpallagolturinn. It keeps the existing music volume and
menu/game music switches.

## Score

- **Sideline Hush** plays in the main menu.
- **Muted trumpet phrases** plays at the planning desk.
- **Muted trumpet phrases (1)** plays during the working week.

## City ambience

- **Cobblestone City Life** and both **1935 Sidewalk Ambience** recordings can
  play as the city sound bed. One is selected at random each time the city
  sound scene starts.
- City ambience streams from its MP3 file rather than being fully decoded into
  memory. Effects, music volume and ambience distance filtering remain in place.

## Windows files

- **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup-0.3.0-beta.4.exe** — Windows installer.
- **RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.4.exe** — portable Windows build.

Existing saves remain compatible. The game clock remains 24 real minutes per
game day.
