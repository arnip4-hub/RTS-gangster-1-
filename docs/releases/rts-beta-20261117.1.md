# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.1

This is a new beta line. The 24-real-minute game day and the seven-day city
calendar are unchanged.

## What's added compared with Original Gangster

- Continuous movement and city activity through day and night, including
  routines for shopkeepers, residents, officials and police shifts.
- Live case-based police and FBI investigations, witness reports, warrants,
  physical arrests, prosecution review and court hearings.
- Investigation and court careers: officers, agents, prosecutors and judges
  now have different saved experience and integrity. Their abilities change
  evidence quality and verdict consistency.
- Vehicle plate leads, recorded detention interviews, independent correction
  of some investigative errors, and finite political delays.
- One lawyer-filed appeal after conviction. It costs $1,250 and can be upheld
  or overturned after one game day.
- Personal and family memories that can affect fear, hostility and witness
  willingness over time.

This remains an experimental fan project built from the separate remake source.
It does not include assets from the 1998 commercial game.

## Other changes in this beta

- Removed **Illustrated 3D** from Game Options because animated clothing
  stretched during movement. Old profiles using it now fall back to **Detailed**.
- Updated the police and court guide with the actual limits of the new systems.
- Retained classic five-day mode and existing save compatibility.
