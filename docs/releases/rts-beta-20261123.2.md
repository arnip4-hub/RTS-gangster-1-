# RTS Gangster 1 — Beta 0.3.0-beta.10

This beta builds on **0.3.0-beta.9** and focuses on clearer building controls, quicker access to the Organizer, and the visible police custody sequence.

## Building dossier and context actions

- The building window now opens with a **Property dossier** showing the property name, type, district and block, identified owner, condition, and value or asking price when known.
- A gang-owned site correctly identifies the gang as its owner; a former seller is distinguished from the current owner.
- Hidden criminal operations and unknown owners remain subject to the player's existing intelligence rather than being revealed by the window.
- Plan site-specific **property and business**, **protection**, **personnel**, and **hostile-operation** jobs directly from the building window.
- Choose a crew and open the existing Organizer job card with the building or known proprietor already targeted. The player must still confirm the order, so simply selecting a command spends no cash or crew time.
- Existing building management actions (manager, trading, repairs, sale, shutdown, freight) remain available.

## Working-week top navigation

- Keep the established oxblood leather header and parchment/brass button design.
- Add **Options** immediately beside **Exit**, matching Exit's styling.
- Add compact shortcuts for **Accountant**, **Lawyer**, **Newspaper**, **Graphs**, and **City maps**.
- Shortcuts open the corresponding Organizer section without stopping the live simulation.
- Preserve the street location, working-week time, cash display, and original lower command bar.
- The Organizer is positioned below the live header so the top-bar shortcuts remain clickable.

## Arrests, escorts and custody

- Arresting officers remain briefly with their detainees at the scene, then escort or transport them to police custody.
- The arrested character no longer stands indefinitely on the pavement under a prison marker after the officer leaves.
- Detainees disappear from the street presentation once transported or booked, while prosecution and custody review continue.
- Interrupted transfers and older beta saves containing stranded arrested people are recovered into custody.
- Prosecutor-requested scene investigations can continue with a second officer while the arresting officer transports the detainee.
- Released detainees are returned to the entrance of the police station.

## Verification and compatibility

- Added tests for context-sensitive building actions, owner privacy, preselected job cards and live top-bar navigation.
- Included the new tests in the Windows build alongside existing targeting, staffing, police, hearings, prison and save-compatibility regressions.
- Existing save-game and installed application identities are retained.
- Both Windows packages must be built and the release published only after tests pass.
- Windows packaging checks that the application and lockfile versions match before generating the installer and portable executable.

- `RTS-gangster-beta-Setup-0.3.0-beta.10.exe` — Windows installer or upgrade.
- `RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.10.exe` — Windows executable that runs without installation.
