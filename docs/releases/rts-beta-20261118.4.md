# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.6

## Lawyer case inquiries and staff transport

- A lawyer's inquiry into one reported crime is a single courthouse visit. It
  takes 30 game minutes after arrival, reveals the lead investigator, and then
  sends the lawyer back to HQ.
- The case panel shows whether the lawyer is travelling or reviewing the file,
  including the 30-minute progress counter. The job no longer persists across
  weeks.
- Lawyers and accountants always use their assigned staff car for work trips.
  Assigning a car remains optional; a specialist without one travels on foot.
- An inquiry that crosses the weekly settlement keeps its actual travel state
  rather than being incorrectly marked as already at the courthouse.

Existing saves remain compatible.
