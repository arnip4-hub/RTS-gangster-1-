# RTS gangster beta — 0.2.0-beta.4

Police and FBI investigations now distinguish what happened from what the
investigators actually know. An unseen killing does not instantly make its true
perpetrator wanted; officers need identification and grounds, then must reach
that person to arrest him.

## Download

- **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup.exe** — normal Windows installer.
- **RTS-gangster-beta-Portable.exe** — portable Windows executable.

The product remains **RTS gangster beta** and keeps the existing save location.
Previous illustrated 3D artwork, controls and upstream 1.0.8 fixes are included.

## Changes

- Saved witness observations distinguish sounds, descriptions, vehicles, faces
  and direct observation. Visibility, walls, distance, darkness and fear matter.
  Civilian reports are delayed; discovering a body can open an unknown-offender
  inquiry. Evidence can reopen a cold investigation.
- Police travel to scenes and witnesses, collect case-specific evidence and
  search last-known locations. Wanted status follows actual warrants. Distant
  suspects and unrelated teammates cannot be arrested by a desk roll.
- FBI inquiries follow reported leads and disclosed records. Agents observe
  actual deliveries before obtaining grounds to search a known site. Searches
  require physical arrival; seized records can identify a manager without
  magically arresting an absent manager or the entire crew.
- Prosecutors review the actual detention file, charge, decline, or request
  further investigation. Initial review is six game hours after arrest, with
  a 24-hour limit for uncharged custody. Investigators can continue scene work
  during that review. Hearings normally follow one or two game days later.
- Court strength follows that file's current evidence and identity. Unrelated
  gang-wide heat is not evidence. Witness withdrawal, unavailable testimony,
  corrupt officials and replacement jurors affect proceedings. Seized financial
  records remain usable even if their source later dies or is imprisoned.
- **Book → Lawyer → Police and Bureau files** shows learned case details,
  received statements, identification, warrants and prosecutor decisions.
  Lawyer inquiries and a paid lead officer disclose files; witness names still
  require court disclosure. Corruption can delay a file, not freeze it forever.
- New law data, custody and physical assignments persist across saves and week
  boundaries. Older RTS court records migrate conservatively; the classic mode
  retains its own rules.

## Clock

**Unchanged:** 24 real minutes per full game day at normal speed, 24 hours a day,
Monday through Sunday. Weekly finances and sentence settlement continue without
resetting the city's people or vehicles.

## Validation and limits

Targeted automated coverage includes police knowledge, witnesses, physical
arrests, FBI surveillance/searches, prosecution, court interference, saves,
week boundaries, kidnapping, prisons, rival AI and the prior upstream fixes.
The Windows workflow reruns the law and upstream-fix suites before packaging.
Production compilation and save-schema validation are required by packaging.

This is a beta investigation model. Descriptions and vehicle observations are
basic; no forensic laboratory, licence-plate investigation, interactive
interrogation or full appeals system is implemented. It needs playtesting and
balance feedback. A successful Windows build is not a Windows playthrough.
See the [law guide](../RTS_LAW.md) for the exact mechanics and limits.
