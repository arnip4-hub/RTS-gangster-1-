# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.5

## Audio mix

- City ambience now has a separate saved volume slider in Options.
- The long city ambience MP3s no longer use the discrete Effects bus. Their
  volume and distance filter are controlled independently from gunshots, UI
  sounds and the music score.
- Random traffic horns now start later, occur much less often, and play at a
  much lower level.
- Music and Effects controls retain their existing behavior and saved values.

The soundtrack and city recordings from beta.4 are unchanged. Existing saves
remain compatible.

## Windows files

- **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup-0.3.0-beta.5.exe** — Windows installer.
- **RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.5.exe** — portable Windows build.
