# RTS gangster beta — 0.3.0-beta.3

This beta expands specialist recruitment and rival intelligence. The agreed
24-real-minute game day and seven-day city calendar are unchanged.

## Accountants and lawyers

- New city recruitment pools are stocked at each venue instead of placing only
  six accountants and six lawyers randomly around town. Accountancy firms,
  finance companies and law offices have three specialists each; the main bank
  and courthouse have five each.
- Vacant recruitment slots refill at the weekly turnover. The original city
  continues to use its venue-specific quotas.
- The Accountant and Lawyer panels list all free specialists, show their
  workplace, distinguish people willing to join from those not yet persuaded,
  and let you hire anyone who has agreed.

## Rival intelligence

- Snitches and information from interrogated rival prisoners prioritize an
  unknown rival accountant or lawyer among the names they reveal.
- Once identified, key staff appear in the rival's Diplomacy details. Buttons
  there open an Assault, Kidnap or Kill order against that person.

## Order planning

- Multi-stop Extort orders keep the selected first property, then visit nearby
  properties in a shorter greedy route instead of zigzagging across town.
- The order list shows whether a crew is travelling, working, waiting, queued
  or finished, and shows the latest recruitment result.
- The People tab in Choose on Map can locate people while a site-based order is
  being planned, without changing that order's target type.

## Windows files

- **RTS-gangster-beta-Setup-0.3.0-beta.3.exe** — Windows installer.
- **RTS-gangster-beta-Portable-0.3.0-beta.3.exe** — portable Windows build.

Existing saves remain compatible. This is an experimental fan project based
on the separate remake source; it does not include assets from the 1998 GOG
game.
