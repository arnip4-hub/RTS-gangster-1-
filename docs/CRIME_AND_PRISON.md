# Crime and prison: current beta and next design

## What the beta already supports

Crews can raid, kidnap, assault, smash premises, torch or bomb a building,
kill a person, and ambush rivals. Police reports retain witnesses and arrest
risk. Arrests lead to court cases with a judge, prosecutor, witnesses and a
jury. A named witness can be bribed or killed; a named juror can be bribed or
targeted, but a dead juror is replaced and the hearing is delayed. A prosecutor
can be bribed for witness and jury names. A lawyer can identify the officer
handling a reported crime by visiting the courthouse. An officer on the gang's
payroll knows the files assigned to him, and his influence affects that
investigation. A killed officer is replaced. Police reports from owners appear
in the Squealers panel, where the player can prepare a hit.

RTS charging reviews and hearings now use live game deadlines; see
[Police, FBI and prosecution](RTS_LAW.md). The classic mode retains weekly trials. Convicted members serve their
sentence in County Jail or the State Penitentiary. Prison contacts and attacks
resolve at a daily morning roll call in RTS mode; the prisons are virtual and
do not have walkable interiors.

## Prison rules in the beta

1. Inmates can only learn about another gang member in the same prison, after
   at least one day of overlap. A paid contact attempt reveals at most one name.
   The source and discovery time persist in the gang's private intelligence.
2. An inmate can buy protection for three days or arrange an attack on a known
   rival inmate. The attempt has a failure chance. Protection reduces its
   success chance; successful attacks can injure or kill, and either outcome
   extends the attacker's sentence and raises evidence.
3. AI inmates sometimes learn a name and occasionally attempt an attack using
   the same knowledge and cash requirements. They do not see the whole prison.
4. Contacts and attacks resolve daily. Sentence settlement remains
   weekly; RTS hearings do not wait for that boundary.

## Later prison depth

Add named wings, guards, visits and rumors with confidence. A lawyer or corrupt
guard could pass a message; a prison killing should open its own criminal case
and let both gangs react. None of those systems is in this beta.

## Other crime gaps worth considering

- **Debt enforcement and labour racketeering:** jobs linked to loans, unions and
  industrial businesses, with financial consequences before adding more combat.

Cargo hijacking and targeted witness pressure are implemented orders. Route
defence, debt enforcement and labour racketeering are proposals.
