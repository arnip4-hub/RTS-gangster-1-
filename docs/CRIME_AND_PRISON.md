# Crime and prison: current beta and next design

## What the beta already supports

Crews can raid, kidnap, assault, smash premises, torch or bomb a building,
kill a person, and ambush rivals. Police reports retain witnesses and arrest
risk. Arrests lead to court cases with a judge, prosecutor, witnesses and a
jury. A named witness can be bribed or killed; a named juror can be bribed or
targeted, but a dead juror is replaced and the hearing is delayed. A prosecutor
can be bribed for witness and jury names. A lawyer can identify the officer
handling a reported crime by visiting the courthouse. An officer on the gang's
payroll knows the files assigned to him, and his influence affects that
investigation. A killed officer is replaced. Police reports from owners appear
in the Squealers panel, where the player can prepare a hit.

The trial still occurs at a weekly boundary. A prison sentence is currently a
week counter on the character; there is no prison population simulation.

## Prison design for the next stage

1. Assign each convicted character to a named prison and a wing. Only people
   who overlap there can meet. Do not reveal all inmates or rival plans to the
   player or AI.
2. Each in-game day, a prisoner may learn a limited fact from an inmate,
   guard, visitor or newspaper. Store who supplied it and a confidence level.
   A rumor can be wrong or stale; a name and location require confirmation.
3. Allow a gang to ask its imprisoned member or lawyer to make contact, seek
   protection or pass a message. A corrupt guard can help for money, but can
   also report the attempt. These actions use time and a real contact path.
4. An attack on an inmate requires a known target, an available accomplice in
   the same prison or a corrupt guard, and a chance to fail. It can injure or
   kill, cause retaliation, extend sentences and create new police/FBI evidence.
   Prison protection competes for the same money and contacts.
5. Resolve prison activity daily. Keep sentence reduction and court accounting
   at their existing weekly boundary until the RTS legal calendar is redesigned.

## Other crime gaps worth considering

- **Cargo hijacking:** intercept an actual truck and its load. This connects
  violence to the freight system and gives rival gangs a reason to defend routes.
- **Targeted witness pressure:** a named, risky alternative to murder or a cash
  bribe, with a chance that the witness reports the intimidation instead.
- **Debt enforcement and labour racketeering:** jobs linked to loans, unions and
  industrial businesses, with financial consequences before adding more combat.

These are proposals, not implemented orders. Prison attacks should follow the
contact and information rules so no gang receives perfect knowledge of inmates.
